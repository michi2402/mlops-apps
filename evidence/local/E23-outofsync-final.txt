--- Analysis (discharges F06/P7.4) ---
Two distinct causes are mixed in the OutOfSync set above:
1. dask, katib, kfp, kfp-crds, kubeflow-spark-operator, monitoring: syncPolicy.automated was
   deliberately disabled this session (INT-03, capacity management for the local profile). Expected
   and self-inflicted -- not a platform finding.
2. lakefs, minio, mlflow, postgres, kafka-operator: automated={"prune":true,"selfHeal":true} is still
   active (confirmed via `kubectl get applications.argoproj.io <app> -o jsonpath='{.spec.syncPolicy.automated}'`)
   and all five are Healthy, yet none is Synced. This is FINDING-01: ArgoCD does not retry an
   automated sync that already failed against the exact same source revision -- confirmed from
   argocd-application-controller logs ("Skipping auto-sync: failed previous sync attempt to [rev],
   will not retry for [rev]"). All five failed their very first sync attempt during the CRD/webhook
   races of INT-01/INT-02 and have not re-synced since, though their live state has long matched
   desired state.

§12.2.1's claim ("precisely the applications that own ExternalSecret resources") is not the right
generalisation -- it is neither necessary (platform-kafka-operator owns no ExternalSecret and is
OutOfSync for reason 2) nor sufficient (platform-external-secrets owns none and is trivially Synced;
platform-monitoring owns one (platform-grafana-secret, per E24) and is OutOfSync for reason 1, not
because of the secret). The true statement: an app is OutOfSync either because an operator has
deliberately paused its automated sync, or because its first sync attempt against the current
revision failed and nothing has since triggered a fresh attempt -- ExternalSecret ownership is
incidental to both.
